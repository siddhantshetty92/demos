# Acme Scheduling Product Guide

## Forecasting
Acme Scheduling forecasts contact volume per queue in 15, 30 or 60 minute intervals from historical actuals, with holiday and promotion adjustments.

## Scheduling
Schedules are generated against forecast requirements and respect shift rules, breaks, meals and agent preferences. Shift bidding is partially supported: agents can rank shift patterns, but seniority weighting is not available.

## Integrations
Acme Scheduling ingests contacts and agent presence from telephony, chat and ticketing platforms through REST APIs and webhooks. A file drop over SFTP is supported for partners that cannot call an API.

## Availability
The service targets 99.9% monthly uptime, published on a public status page.
